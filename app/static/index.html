1 · Upload your complete steel dataset to train automatically

Selecting a valid CSV starts preprocessing, chronological train/validation/test splitting, Random Forest and XGBoost tuning, test evaluation, and model activation. Required columns include date, Usage_kWh, reactive-power and power-factor readings, NSM, WeekStatus, Day_of_week, and Load_Type.

Training requires the access key and persistent MongoDB storage configured by the project owner.
